Accessibility Is an Ethical AI Requirement, Not a Polish Pass

If your AI UI fails basic accessibility, you are excluding users while claiming responsible AI. Bake a11y checks into the ethical release gate.

Why this belongs in the ethics lane

Fairness is not only demographic parity in model scores. An assistant that cannot be operated by keyboard or screen reader fails equal access. Treat that as a release blocker alongside safety probes.

Minimum AI-UI a11y gate

Keyboard path for primary ask-and-answer flow, visible focus, labels on inputs, status messages announced to assistive tech, and contrast that meets your policy. Automate axe or equivalent in CI; keep one manual screen-reader pass per release.

For generated content, ensure the UI does not rely on color alone to show risk or refusal state.

Owners

Product owns the standard; QA owns the gate evidence; design owns component fixes. Put the a11y evidence link in the same release packet as your ethical checklist.
