When the Vendor Swaps the Model: An Ethics Checklist for QA

Provider silent upgrades change behavior without a PR. Treat model swaps like dependency upgrades with ethical side effects.

You did not ship the change—but you own the impact

If your app calls a floating alias (latest, gpt-x), the vendor can move the target. Tone, refusal boundaries, and tool-calling habits shift overnight. Users still blame your product.

Pin versions in production. Allow aliases only in non-prod. When you must move, run the same ethical release checklist you use for your own prompt changes.

Checklist before accepting a new model id

Diff safety and over-refusal scores on your fixed suites. Re-run bias and PII probes. Confirm logging still captures model id and version. Update the risk register entry with the swap date and owner.

If any blocker fails, keep the pin and open a vendor ticket—do not paper over it with a hotter temperature.

Communicate

Tell Support and Trust & Safety what changed and how to escalate weird refusals or new failure modes for two weeks after the cutover.
